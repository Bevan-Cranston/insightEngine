# Philosophy

## Goal
Insight Engine is designed to leverage the unique capabilities of large language models to support long-term self-actualization.

## Background
LLMs benefit from use of language as a system of representing ground truth. Language, metaphor and narrative are fluid, 
adaptive symbolic systems - they are imprecise but profoundly deep, capable of pointing to truths that numbers and 
equations alone cannot. Mathematics and code are highly structured symbolic systems - they are precise but limited because they 
cannot capture subjective experience, emotion or emergent meaning.

## Features
Adaptability. Insight Engine will be architected in such a way that it can adapt itself to idiosyncrasies of each user. 
By use of adaptive memory, the system will work as a coevolving intelligence that reflects, refines and structures the 
user's thoughts. It will use this fluid adaptable memory to amplify the user's self reflection, reinforcing key 
breakthroughs, and helping to indentify recurring themes and sticking points.

Long-term recal. Where traditional LLM chat systems lose the most valuable insights across different conversations and 
long message history. Insight engine's memory will be structured to rank the most important breakthroughs to retrieve at
key moments in the conversation to augment a current reflection and help the LLM to customize to reflection to the
user's journey, making sure that important insights (often gathered through the most challenging times in the user's journey) are maximally leveraged 
for their growth.

Sequencing. Reflections will be designed to work on multiple timeframes e.g. daily reminders and affirmations, weekly 
debriefs and even longer term strategic revisits to the most important milestone breakthroughs. Time and date based 
awareness will be used to trigger specific memory retrieval patterns that relate to each layer of sequencing.

Insight Engine is designed to augment rather than replace human reflection. The role of the system is to organise memory, 
surface relevant context and encourage deeper thinking while leaving interpretation and decision-making with the user. 
System prompts will be structured in such a way to encourage the LLM to function in this way.
