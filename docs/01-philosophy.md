# Philosophy

## Goal
Insight Engine is designed to leverage the unique capabilities of large language models to support long-term 
self-actualization.

## Product Vision
As gym equipment is used to structure physical effort and maximise the return on this effort to the user, Insight Engine
is designed to structure the mental effort a user puts towards their own cognitive and emotional growth and maximize the
return on this effort to the user.

## Background
LLMs benefit from the use of language as a system of representing ground truth. Language, metaphor and narrative are 
fluid, adaptive symbolic systems - they are imprecise but profoundly deep, capable of pointing to truths that numbers 
and equations alone cannot. Mathematics and code are highly structured symbolic systems - they are precise but limited 
as a base system for representing subjective experience, emotion or emergent meaning.

## Features
Adaptability. Insight Engine will be architected in such a way that it can adapt itself to the idiosyncrasies of each 
user. By use of adaptive memory, the system will work as a coevolving intelligence that reflects, refines and structures 
the user's thoughts. It will use this fluid adaptable memory to amplify the user's self reflection, reinforcing key 
breakthroughs, and helping to identify recurring themes and sticking points.

Long-term recall. Where traditional LLM chat systems lose the most valuable insights across different conversations and 
long message history. Insight engine's memory will be structured to rank the most important breakthroughs to retrieve at
key moments in the conversation to augment a current reflection and help the LLM to customize the reflection to the
user's journey, making sure that important insights (often gathered through periods of significant challenge) are maximally leveraged for their growth.

Sequencing. Reflections will be designed to work on multiple timeframes e.g. daily reminders and affirmations, weekly 
debriefs and even longer term strategic revisits to the most important milestone breakthroughs. Time and date based 
awareness will be used to trigger specific memory retrieval patterns that relate to each layer of sequencing.

## Key Context

Insight Engine is designed to augment rather than replace human reflection. The role of the system is to organise memory, 
surface relevant context and encourage deeper thinking while leaving interpretation and decision-making with the user. 
System prompts will be structured in such a way to encourage the LLM to function in this way.
